🚀 Start Investing

A simple, directive guide for Australian young adults. Follow the 5 steps, pick your risk profile, and you'll know exactly which ETFs to buy.

Built on Dad's coaching framework.

Your 5-Step Journey

Click each step to expand. Complete them in order.

1
Build Your Safety Net First
+

Don't invest until you have an emergency fund.

What you need:

  • Minimum: $2,000–$3,000 for unexpected expenses
  • Target: 3–6 months of living expenses
  • Where: High-interest savings account (UBank, ING, Macquarie)

Money buckets (from Dad's framework):

Your income splits into buckets:

  1. Fixed expenses — rent, food, transport (cover first)
  2. Emergency fund — build to target, then maintain
  3. Big goal — car, trip, house deposit (optional)
  4. Investments — ETFs for long-term growth (~10–15% of leftover)
  5. Flexible spending — fun, lifestyle, buffer (rest)

💡 Use the Student Wealth Builder calculator to estimate your expenses and see bucket allocations.

2
Open Betashares Direct (Individual)
+

You're an adult, so open a Betashares Direct Individual account (not Kids).

What to do:

  1. Go to direct.betashares.com.au
  2. Sign up for an Individual account
  3. Quote your Tax File Number (TFN) — avoids extra withholding tax
  4. Complete identity verification
Why Betashares Direct?
  • $0 brokerage on ASX ETFs (Manual DIY mode)
  • Fractional investing (buy with any dollar amount)
  • Auto-Invest option for set-and-forget
Note: Betashares Direct is custodial (no personal HIN). Your holdings are safe and segregated. If you want CHESS later, consider Stake or CMC Invest.
3
Fund Your Account
+

Set up recurring bank transfers to your Betashares Direct Wallet.

How to fund:

  1. In Betashares Direct, find your Wallet BSB + account number
  2. Set up recurring bank transfer from your bank (weekly/fortnightly/monthly)
  3. Osko often makes BSB transfers instant
  4. Start small ($50–$200/month is fine)

Note: PayID is not advertised on Betashares Direct — use BSB + account number.

4
Pick Your Risk Profile & Buy ETFs
+

This is the key step. Choose your risk profile below, then buy the exact ETFs listed.

Scroll down to the "Choose Your Allocation" section to see Conservative, Balanced, or Growth options with specific tickers and percentages.

5
Automate & Leave Alone
+

How to automate:

  • Manual DIY (recommended while small): Buy manually each month when your transfer arrives — takes 2 minutes, $0 fees
  • Auto-Invest: Set up if all your ETFs are Betashares (up to 5 ETFs, fixed $ amounts)
  • Custom Portfolio: Use only if you want % auto-rebalancing and accept ~$3–4.50/month fee (better for $10k+ balances)

Then leave it alone:

  • Review once a year
  • Don't check daily — time in the market beats timing the market
  • Keep contributing regularly (dollar-cost averaging)

Lower Risk

Conservative

More bonds, lower volatility. Long-term focus with smoother ride.

Who it's for:

You want steady growth with less ups and downs. You're nervous about big market swings or need money in 5–10 years.

Exact allocation:

AAA 30%
QPON 20%
CRED 15%
WBND 15%
A200 10%
BGBL 10%

Note: Bonds (AAA, QPON, CRED, WBND) = lower risk, lower return. Shares (A200, BGBL) = higher growth potential.

💰 How to buy (example: $100/month)

  1. In Betashares Direct, search AAA → buy $30
  2. Search QPON → buy $20
  3. Search CRED → buy $15
  4. Search WBND → buy $15
  5. Search A200 → buy $10
  6. Search BGBL → buy $10

Manual DIY = $0 brokerage. Scale these amounts up/down for your monthly contribution.

⚠️ Risk warning: Even conservative portfolios can lose value in the short term. Don't invest money you need in the next 3–5 years.
Neutral Risk

Balanced

Mix of shares and bonds. Default for most young investors.

Who it's for:

Most young adults (~18–30). You want growth but don't want 100% shares. Long-term investor (10+ years) who can handle normal market ups and downs.

Standard Balanced:

BGBL 45%
A200 30%
CRED 15%
AAA 10%

Ultra-Simple 2-ETF Balanced:

If you want the simplest possible path:

BGBL 60%
A200 40%

Benefit: Only 2 ETFs to track. Easy to maintain. Still diversified.

Mixed-issuer alternative:

VAS (Vanguard Australian shares) + VGS (Vanguard global shares) — same concept, different issuer. Requires manual buys (Vanguard not in Betashares Auto-Invest).

💰 How to buy Standard Balanced (example: $100/month)

  1. Search BGBL → buy $45
  2. Search A200 → buy $30
  3. Search CRED → buy $15
  4. Search AAA → buy $10

💰 How to buy Ultra-Simple (example: $100/month)

  1. Search BGBL → buy $60
  2. Search A200 → buy $40

That's it! Easiest to automate with Auto-Invest.

⚠️ Risk warning: Shares can fall 20–40% in bad markets. You must be comfortable holding through downturns.
Higher Risk

Growth

More volatile, higher growth potential. Long time horizon required.

Who it's for:

Young investors (20s–early 30s) with 15+ year horizon. You can stomach big swings and want maximum growth. You won't panic-sell in a crash.

Exact allocation:

NDQ 35%
BGBL 25%
ASIA 15%
A200 15%
BEMG 10%

What this is: Tech-heavy (NDQ = NASDAQ), global diversification, emerging markets. No bonds = higher volatility.

Optional crypto add-on:

If you insist on Bitcoin exposure: QBTC max 1–3% only. Very high risk. Not required for growth portfolio.

💰 How to buy (example: $100/month)

  1. Search NDQ → buy $35
  2. Search BGBL → buy $25
  3. Search ASIA → buy $15
  4. Search A200 → buy $15
  5. Search BEMG → buy $10

Optional: Add $1–3 to QBTC if you want Bitcoin (max 3% total allocation).

⚠️ Risk warning: This portfolio can fall 40–60% in severe markets. Tech (NDQ) and emerging markets (ASIA, BEMG) are volatile. Only use if you won't panic-sell and have 15+ years before you need the money.

📚 Key Terms (Click to Expand)

+

ETF (Exchange Traded Fund): A basket of investments that trades on the stock exchange. When you buy VGS, you own a slice of ~1,400 global companies.

Ticker: The ASX code for an ETF (e.g., A200, BGBL, NDQ).

MER (Management Expense Ratio): Annual fee as % of investment. Example: A200 = 0.04% p.a. = $0.40/year on $1,000.

Fractional shares: Buy less than 1 full unit. Betashares Direct supports this.

DCA (Dollar-Cost Averaging): Invest a fixed amount regularly (e.g., $200/month). Smooths out price swings.

Custodial vs CHESS: Betashares = custodial (no personal HIN). CHESS = shares in your name with HIN (Holder Identification Number). Both are safe; CHESS offers more portability.

TFN (Tax File Number): Quote it when opening accounts to avoid extra withholding tax.

Franking credits: Tax credit for Australian shares (applies to A200, VAS). Can boost after-tax returns for low-income earners.
